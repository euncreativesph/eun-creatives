About me

I combine creativity with IT skills to bring ideas to life.

I'm a website developer, graphic designer and video editor. I've been editing video for six years and designing for five. It started with social media graphics and grew into branding, packaging, website design and development, and a steady stream of short-form edits for creators and brands.

Design and editing feed each other. A designer's eye makes my edits cleaner, and editing teaches me what makes people stop and look. I'm currently studying Information Technology at Our Lady of Fatima University, which keeps me fluent in the digital side of the work.
